The company I chose to research that was built upon sequencing technologies is 23&me.
The founding mission of 23&Me was to help consumers understand, access, and benefit from the human genome.
23&Me hopes to build a different kind of healthcare system by using the human genome.
The primary co-founder of 23&Me is Anne Wojcicki. However, there are two other co-founders named: Linda Avey and Paul Cusenza. 
23&Me was founded in April of 2006. In May 2007, 23&Me reported $3.9 million mostly from Google. 
In March 2025, 23&Me filed for bankruptcy and sold the company to a nonprofit called TTAM Research Institute. The nonprofit reported
the revenue to be $170 million dollars. 
23&Me has a business focus of personal genetics, consumer DNA testing, and biopharmaceutical research. 
23&Me currently is in a shift to run as a nonprofit. As for the future, 23&Me plans to focus on research to expand a genetic database. This massive database 
is used for scientific studies to build upon for research such as obesity and cannabis.
At their core, 23&Me will still sell DNA kits to consumers for ancestry.
