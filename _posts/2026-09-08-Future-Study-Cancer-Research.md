Leukemia is the cancer of tissue responsible for forming the body's blood such as bone marrow and the lymphatic system. 
Generally, Leukemia is very closely connected with white blood cells. White blood cells are a strong line of defense for the body.
Usually, Leukemia makes those white blood cells divide out of control and not be able to function properly. 
Leukemia has a freqeuncy of about 14.7 in 100,000 males and females.
Leukemia is detected by a combination of physical exams, blood marrow tests, as well as blood tests. Physical exams can include looking for signs of anemia, 
swollen lymph nodes, and enlarged spleen and/or liver. 
The exact reasons are not yet known, but Leukemia seems to stem from a mix of external environmental factors as well as genetic predisposition. 
Leukemia does not have a typical staging arrangement. However, some types of leukemia do follow the usual stage I-IV, but other subtypes do not. There are loop
several subtypes of leukemia.T
The current and most prevanlent treatment of leukemia and its subtypes is chemotherapy.However, there are many other treatment plans such as radiation therapy, 
immunotherapy, targeted therapy, bone marrow transplant, engineered immune cells, and even clinical trials.
The average life expectancy of an indvidual with Leukemia is about a 68.6% five year survival rate.
